# Individual Trainer Declaration Form (NIN Path)

Shown to any applicant applying as an **Individual Trainer** without a CAC-registered business. Presented as a required, checkbox-gated form step during application (§3.1 of the blueprint), alongside NIN capture and a government-issued photo ID upload. This is drafted as a strong starting point — have it reviewed by a lawyer before it governs real applications, since it carries legal/attestation weight.

---

## STATUTORY DECLARATION AND SELF-ATTESTATION

**Applicant Full Legal Name:** _______________________
**National Identification Number (NIN):** _______________________
**Date:** _______________________

I, the undersigned, being the individual named above, do hereby solemnly and sincerely declare as follows:

1. I am the person I claim to be, and the National Identification Number and government-issued identification document provided in this application are genuine, accurate, and belong to me.

2. I possess genuine competence, training, qualification, and/or verifiable practical experience in the field(s) of training I have indicated in this application, and I am not misrepresenting my ability to deliver such training.

3. All information I have provided in this application — including my contact details, physical address, and description of the training I intend to certify — is true and correct to the best of my knowledge, and I have not knowingly omitted or falsified any material fact.

4. I understand that any certificate I issue through Certified will represent to the public, including prospective employers, that the certified individual has genuinely undergone and completed the training described, under my direct supervision or instruction.

5. I understand and accept that Certified relies on this declaration, in the absence of formal business registration, as a basis for approving my account, and that this declaration does not itself constitute verification of my training competence by Certified — it is my own sworn representation.

6. I understand that knowingly making a false declaration for the purpose of obtaining approval on this platform may constitute an offence under applicable Nigerian law, including but not limited to provisions of the Cybercrimes (Prohibition, Prevention, etc.) Act and the Criminal Code/Penal Code as applicable, and may result in immediate suspension of my account, removal of all certificates issued by me, and referral to appropriate authorities where warranted.

7. I understand that Certified, its administrators, and its agents reserve the right to request additional supporting evidence of my training competence at any time, to suspend or revoke my approved status if such evidence is not satisfactorily provided, and to disclose the revocation (with reason) on any certificate I have issued.

8. I consent to Certified retaining this declaration, my NIN, and my identification document for verification, audit, dispute-resolution, and regulatory compliance purposes, in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and Certified's Privacy Policy.

By checking the box below and submitting this application, I confirm that I have read, understood, and agree to the above declaration in its entirety.

☐ **I have read and agree to this declaration.**
