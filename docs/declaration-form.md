# Individual Trainer Declaration Form

Shown to any applicant applying as an **Individual Trainer** without a formally registered business (e.g. CAC-registered in Nigeria, or the equivalent registration elsewhere). Presented as a required, checkbox-gated form step during application (§3.1 of the blueprint), alongside the identification document upload (any means of identification — business/work ID card, government-issued ID, etc.). This is drafted as a strong starting point — have it reviewed by a lawyer (ideally one familiar with the applicant's country of operation, given the platform's pan-African scope) before it governs real applications, since it carries legal/attestation weight.

---

## STATUTORY DECLARATION AND SELF-ATTESTATION

**Applicant Full Legal Name:** _______________________
**Country of Operation:** _______________________
**Date:** _______________________

I, the undersigned, being the individual named above, do hereby solemnly and sincerely declare as follows:

1. I am the person I claim to be, and the identification document provided in this application is genuine, accurate, and belongs to me.

2. I possess genuine competence, training, qualification, and/or verifiable practical experience in the field(s) of training I have indicated in this application, and I am not misrepresenting my ability to deliver such training.

3. All information I have provided in this application — including my contact details, physical address, and description of the training I intend to certify — is true and correct to the best of my knowledge, and I have not knowingly omitted or falsified any material fact.

4. I understand that any certificate I issue through Certified Africa will represent to the public, including prospective employers, that the certified individual has genuinely undergone and completed the training described, under my direct supervision or instruction.

5. I understand and accept that Certified Africa relies on this declaration, in the absence of formal business registration, as a basis for approving my account, and that this declaration does not itself constitute verification of my training competence by Certified Africa — it is my own sworn representation.

6. I understand that knowingly making a false declaration for the purpose of obtaining approval on this platform may constitute an offence under applicable law in my country of operation, including but not limited to cybercrime, fraud, and criminal-law provisions in force there, and may result in immediate suspension of my account, removal of all certificates issued by me, and referral to appropriate authorities where warranted.

7. I understand that Certified Africa, its administrators, and its agents reserve the right to request additional supporting evidence of my training competence at any time, to suspend or revoke my approved status if such evidence is not satisfactorily provided, and to disclose the revocation (with reason) on any certificate I have issued.

8. I consent to Certified Africa retaining this declaration and my identification document for verification, audit, dispute-resolution, and regulatory compliance purposes, in accordance with the data protection law applicable in my country of operation (for example, Nigeria's Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, or the equivalent law elsewhere) and Certified Africa's Privacy Policy.

By checking the box below and submitting this application, I confirm that I have read, understood, and agree to the above declaration in its entirety.

☐ **I have read and agree to this declaration.**
